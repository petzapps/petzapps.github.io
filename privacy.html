Privacy Policy

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used only by its developer to upload the developer's own videos to the developer's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

The app requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload) solely to upload videos. It does not read, collect, or store any other user data.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the developer's computer. No data is collected from other users, shared with third parties, or sold.
